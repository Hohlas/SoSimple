All experiments are launched via `python -m ML.<script>` from the repository root after activating `.venv`; common workflows include `train --task regression_updn|triple_barrier|entry_path_v1|take_skip_v2`, `optimize` with Optuna trials, `evaluate_test` for OOS scoring, and task-specific runners like `run_entry_path_live_safe_retrain` and `run_take_skip_original_contour_feature_matrix` that accept `--seeds`, `--epochs`, `--batch-size`, and `--clear-cache` flags. Artifacts are written under `ML/checkpoints/`, `ML/plots/`, and `ML/reports/<experiment_name>/seed_<i>/` following a deterministic seed-based directory convention.
